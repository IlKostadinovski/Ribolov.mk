# Sources for the fishing information

Every fact in `data/waters.json` comes from one of the sources below. Nothing was added
from memory or from unverified websites (forums, fishing blogs, AI-written pages).

## Rules used

1. **Fish species** are listed for a water only if a reliable source names them *for that
   specific water*: an official fishery management plan (Риболовна основа), a
   peer-reviewed scientific paper, or a documented field survey.
2. For the three natural lakes (Ohrid, Prespa, Dojran), where both a plan and a recent
   scientific survey exist, a species is listed only if **both** agree, or the plan lists
   it as a current inhabitant and no other source contradicts it.
3. Species that a source says are **no longer present, very rare or not confirmed** are
   left out (examples are given per water below).
4. Uncertain identifications (written "sp." or "cf." in a source) are left out.
5. Tags: **"endemic"** only where a source calls the species endemic to that lake or its
   basin; **"introduced"** only where a source says so.
6. Names: the scientific (Latin) name as written in the source; the Macedonian name from
   the official plans (народно име); the English name only where a source gives one or it
   is the standard common name of a widespread European species.
7. Permits, prices, closed seasons, minimum sizes and catch limits were **not** added,
   even where plans contain them: they have legal consequences and should be checked with
   the fishing associations or the Ministry.

## Sources

### Official fishery management plans (Риболовни основи) 2023–2028
Prepared by the Institute of Animal Science (Институт за сточарство), Ss. Cyril and
Methodius University in Skopje, adopted by the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Water
Economy under Art. 16(3) of the Law on Fisheries and Aquaculture. Each plan has a species
table (section 5.1) with the Latin name (after Kottelat & Freyhof 2007), synonyms and the
Macedonian folk name, followed by a description of each species that inhabits the water.

- **Охридско Езеро** – http://istoc.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/istoc/1.%20%D0%9E%D0%A5%D0%A0%D0%98%D0%94%D0%A1%D0%9A%D0%9E%20%D0%95%D0%97%D0%95%D0%A0%D0%9E%202023-2028.pdf
  Used for: Lake Ohrid species (table 3 + species descriptions), Macedonian names, lake
  facts (693.17 m, 358.18 km², 238.79 km² in North Macedonia, max depth 288.7 m, mean
  163.1 m, fed by sublacustrine springs, outflow Crn Drim at Struga), shad no longer reaching
  the lake (dams on the Drim); Sateska facts (diverted into the lake in 1961, 26 km to the
  regulating dam near Volino, canal ~6 km, lamprey lives in the Sateska).
  Left out for Ohrid: twaite shad (no longer present); rainbow trout, topmouth gudgeon,
  pumpkinseed (not found in the 2016 survey of the lake, see below); bitterling (named
  differently in the table and the description); gambusia, Salmo farioides, Eudontomyzon
  mariae (no description for the lake / tributaries only); Eudontomyzon "sp."
- **Преспанско Езеро** – http://istoc.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/istoc/2.%20%D0%9F%D0%A0%D0%95%D0%A1%D0%9F%D0%90%D0%9D%D0%A1%D0%9A%D0%9E%20%D0%95%D0%97%D0%95%D0%A0%D0%9E%202023-2028%20.pdf
  Used for: cross-check of the Prespa species, Macedonian names, lake facts (853 m,
  274 km²: 177 MK / 49 AL / 48 GR, max depth 76 m, 28 × 17 km, large level changes),
  Prespa trout coming into the lake from the Brajčinska and Leva rivers.
  The plan itself says Ohrid trout, rainbow trout, grass carp, silver carp, minnow and
  gambusia no longer occur in the lake.
- **Дојранско Езеро** – http://istoc.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/istoc/3.%20%D0%94%D0%9E%D0%88%D0%A0%D0%90%D0%9D%D0%A1%D0%9A%D0%9E%20%D0%95%D0%97%D0%95%D0%A0%D0%9E%202023-2028.pdf
  Used for: cross-check of the Dojran species, Macedonian names, most common species
  (roach, perch, Prussian carp, bleak), species now very rare or absent (Balkan barbel,
  gudgeon, chub, golden loach; river blenny not caught for over 40 years), "third largest
  natural lake", ~3/5 of the lake in North Macedonia, present area ~31 km².

### Scientific papers and studies

- **Pietrock, M., Ritterbusch, D., Lewin, W.-C., Shumka, S., Spirkovski, Z., Ilik-Boeva, D.,
  Brämick, U. & Peveling, R. (2022).** The fish community of the ancient Prespa Lake
  (Southeast Europe): Non-indigenous species take over. *Fisheries & Aquatic Life* 30(3):
  112–124. https://doi.org/10.2478/aopf-2022-0011
  Used for: the 15 Prespa species caught with gillnets in 2013–2015 (Macedonian and
  Albanian parts), endemic/native/introduced status, English names, introduced species
  outnumbering native ones.
- **Shumka, S., Petriki, O., Shumka, L. & Berberi, E. (2025).** Fish Fauna, Ecological
  Quality and Conservation Challenges in the Balkan Transboundary Lake Dojran. *Fishes*
  10(6): 272. https://doi.org/10.3390/fishes10060272
  Used for: Dojran species recorded in 2021 (Macedonian part) and 2023 (Greek part):
  Alburnus macedonicus, Perca fluviatilis, Rutilus rutilus, Rhodeus meridionalis,
  Silurus glanis and Cobitis vardarensis (rare); established Carassius gibelio and Tinca
  tinca; carp as a main catch species.
  Left out for Dojran (not clearly stated as caught): rudd, Macedonian roach, silver and
  bighead carp, eel, pike, gambusia.
- **Talevski, T., Talevska, M. & Talevska Leshoska, A. (2025).** Endemic Fish Species in
  the Macedonian Fresh Water. In: *Endemic Species from around the World*. IntechOpen.
  https://doi.org/10.5772/intechopen.1008520
  Used for: confirmation of the endemic species of Lake Ohrid, English names of Ohrid and
  Prespa species, "Dojranska plashica" for Alburnus macedonicus.
- **Trichkova, T., Todorov, M., Botev, I., Trajanovski, S., Talevski, T., Spirkovski, Z. &
  Zdraveski, K.** Inventory of alien fish species in Lake Ohrid catchment in 2016
  (ESENIAS poster). https://www.esenias.org/files/News_Alien_CSI_2_Poster_Ohrid_fish.pdf
  Used for: no alien species found in Lake Ohrid itself in May 2016; Carassius gibelio
  recorded in 2013.
- **UNDP North Macedonia (2025).** Biodiversity study for the restoration of Sateska river
  in its natural riverbed (team leader V. Slavevska Stamenkovic).
  https://www.undp.org/sites/g/files/zskgke326/files/2026-04/biodiversity_study_for_the_restoration_of_sateska_river_in_its_natural_riverbed-final_.pdf
  Used for: Sateska species found in the May–June 2025 field survey; river facts (largest
  tributary in the Ohrid catchment, rises in Debarca, diverted in 1961).
  Left out: "Phoxinus sp." and "Squalius cf. platiceps" (uncertain identifications).
- **Karadelev, M., Kostov, V. & Manevska, I. (2020).** ESIA Kicevo–Bukojcani, Appendix 15.2
  Fisheries Assessment. https://roads.org.mk/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/ESIA-Appendix-15_2-Fisheries-Assessment.pdf
  Used for: Zajaska Reka species caught by electrofishing on 22 October 2020.

### International bodies

- **IUCN World Heritage Outlook – Natural and Cultural Heritage of the Ohrid region.**
  https://worldheritageoutlook.iucn.org/node/968
  Used for: inscribed 1979, extended to Albania 2019, 17 native fish species of which 10
  endemic.
- **Ramsar Information Sheet – Dojran Lake (site no. 1735).** https://rsis.ramsar.org/ris/1735
  and the RIS document https://rsisd10.unepgrid.ch/RISapp/files/RISrep/MK1735RIS.pdf
  Used for: designated 2007, depth about 6 m, Monument of Nature since 1977, traditional
  fishing with "mandras" (the only known place in the world using this method).
- **Ramsar – North Macedonia designates second Ramsar site.**
  https://ramsar.org/fr/news/fyr-macedonia-designates-second-ramsar-site
  Used for: Macedonian part of Lake Prespa designated in 1995.
