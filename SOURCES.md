# Sources for the fishing information

Every fact in `data/waters.json` comes from one of the sources below. Nothing was added
from memory or from unverified websites (forums, fishing blogs, AI-written pages).

## Rules used

1. **Fish species** are listed for a water only if a reliable source names them *for that
   specific water*: an official fishery management plan (Риболовна основа), a
   peer-reviewed scientific paper, or a documented field survey.
2. For the three natural lakes (Ohrid, Prespa, Dojran), where both a plan and a recent
   scientific survey exist, a species is listed only if **both** agree, or the plan lists
   it as a current inhabitant and no other source contradicts it.
3. Species that a source says are **no longer present, very rare or not confirmed** are
   left out (examples are given per water below).
4. Uncertain identifications (written "sp." or "cf." in a source) are left out.
5. Tags: **"endemic"** only where a source calls the species endemic to that lake or its
   basin; **"introduced"** only where a source says so.
6. Names: the scientific (Latin) name as written in the source (obvious misspellings
   corrected, e.g. "Ctenopharingodon" → Ctenopharyngodon, "Gimnocephalus" → Gymnocephalus;
   "Stizostedion lucioperca" is given under its current name Sander lucioperca, which the
   plan lists as the synonym); the Macedonian name from the official plans (народно име);
   the English name only where a source gives one or it is the standard common name of a
   widespread European species. A fish without a name in the chosen language is shown with
   its Latin name.
7. Permits, prices, closed seasons, minimum sizes and catch limits were **not** added,
   even where plans contain them: they have legal consequences and should be checked with
   the fishing associations or the Ministry.
8. **Reservoirs with only a management plan** (Mladost, Streževo): only species that the
   plan names *specifically for that reservoir* in its own text (the reservoir's list of
   main species, or a sentence about that reservoir) are listed, not every species in the
   plan's table. The plans describe each species with general text copied between plans,
   so the general text alone is not taken as proof for a specific water.
9. **Surveys** count for the water where the fish were actually caught: for the Crna Reka
   only the river sampling sites, for the Tikveš reservoir only the reservoir site.
10. The plans call carp (Cyprinus carpio) introduced, but the scientific sources used here
    do not, so carp has no tag.
11. **Waters with only a gazette plan** (see "Official Gazette" below): a species is listed
    only if it is both in the plan's species table and in the plan's table of main species
    for that fishing area ("позначајни видови"). Where the plan has a separate species
    table for a zone (Mavrovo Lake, Radika), that zone table is used.
12. A fishing area often covers more than one water. A **reservoir** gets a fish list only
    if the area is just the reservoir, or if the plan names the species for the reservoir
    itself; otherwise the area's main species are given in a note, clearly saying they are
    for the whole area. A **river** whose area also includes reservoirs, ponds or marshes
    gets only species that are not likely to live only in those still waters (so carp,
    perch, rudd and similar are left out there unless a source puts them in the river).
13. Facts on which two sources disagree are left out (e.g. the length of the Crna Reka:
    222 km in one source, 207 km in the official plan; the length of the Treska: 138 km in the Treska plan as read by OCR vs
    128 km in the middle Vardar plan; the height of the Kozjak dam: 126.1 m vs 114 m in the same plan).

## Sources

### Official fishery management plans (Риболовни основи) 2023–2028
Prepared by the Institute of Animal Science (Институт за сточарство), Ss. Cyril and
Methodius University in Skopje, adopted by the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Water
Economy under Art. 16(3) of the Law on Fisheries and Aquaculture. Each plan has a species
table (section 5.1) with the Latin name (after Kottelat & Freyhof 2007), synonyms and the
Macedonian folk name, followed by a description of each species that inhabits the water.

- **Охридско Езеро** – http://istoc.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/istoc/1.%20%D0%9E%D0%A5%D0%A0%D0%98%D0%94%D0%A1%D0%9A%D0%9E%20%D0%95%D0%97%D0%95%D0%A0%D0%9E%202023-2028.pdf
  Used for: Lake Ohrid species (table 3 + species descriptions), Macedonian names, lake
  facts (693.17 m, 358.18 km², 238.79 km² in Macedonia, max depth 288.7 m, mean
  163.1 m, fed by sublacustrine springs, outflow Crn Drim at Struga), shad no longer reaching
  the lake (dams on the Drim); Sateska facts (diverted into the lake in 1961, 26 km to the
  regulating dam near Volino, canal ~6 km, lamprey lives in the Sateska).
  Left out for Ohrid: twaite shad (no longer present); rainbow trout, topmouth gudgeon,
  pumpkinseed (not found in the 2016 survey of the lake, see below); bitterling (named
  differently in the table and the description); gambusia, Salmo farioides, Eudontomyzon

- **Преспанско Езеро** – http://istoc.ukim.edu.mk/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/istoc/2.%20%D0%9F%D0%A0%D0%95%D0%A1%D0%9F%D0%90%D0%9D%D0%A1%D0%9A%D0%9E%20%D0%95%D0%97%D0%95%D0%A0%D0%9E%202023-2028%20.pdf

### Fish sizes (only used to sort the fish lists, not shown on the site)

`data/fish-sizes.json` gives each species a maximum length in cm, so the lists can show
the biggest fish first. Value = the bigger of:
- the maximum length on the species' FishBase summary page (https://www.fishbase.se/summary/Genus-species.html,
  read on 30 October 2026; standard length × 1.2 and fork length × 1.05 to compare with
  total length), and
- the maximum length given in the species description of the 2023–2028 fishery
  management plans (these describe the species in general, e.g. chub "up to 80 cm").
FishBase had no page for Chondrostoma ohridanus under that name; the Lake Ohrid plan
gives 50 cm. The raw FishBase values are in the `research` branch
(`research/fishbase/sizes.tsv`).

### Official Gazette: the adopted fishery management plans (2024 and 2025)

The plans as adopted by the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Water Economy under the
new Law on Fisheries and Aquaculture (Сл. весник 64/24), published in the Official Gazette
(Службен весник). Each one has: basic data on the fishing area, the species table
(section 2.1), daily catch limits, the spawning table of "the more important species" of
the area (section 4), stocking, guards and protected zones. The PDFs are scans; their text
was read with OCR (in the `research` branch, `research/text/…ocr.txt`) and every species
and number used here was checked by hand against the OCR text.

- **Issue no. 222 of 31 October 2024** –
  https://www.mrf1952.mk/images/documents/ribolovni-osnovi-2024/Ribolovni-osnovi_Sl-vesnik-RM-222.pdf
  (Ohrid, Špilje, Mavrovica, Vardar upper/middle/lower, Treska, Crn Drim, Crna Reka,
  Bregalnica, Pčinja)
- **Issue no. 33 of 14 February 2025** –
  https://www.mrf1952.mk/images/documents/ribolovni-osnovi-2024/Ribolovni-osnovi_Sl-vesnik-RM-33.pdf
  (Mantovo, Ratevo–Berovo Lake, Prespa, Gradče, Konče, Mladost, Dojran, Turija, Vodoča,
  Mavrovo National Park, Kozjak, Strumica, Glažnja, Streževo, Lipkovo, Kalimanci)

Used for (per water):
